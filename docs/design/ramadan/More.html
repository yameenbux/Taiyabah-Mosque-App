Ladies’ arrangements

Dropped

The masjid has no women’s facilities, so there is nothing to publish. A screen listing arrangements that do not exist would send people to a locked door.

It does raise the worth of taraweeh on the stream: for women, and for anyone housebound, that is the only way to hear it.

One thing to know about the hadith

I said earlier that al-Bukhārī is bundled in the app. It is not. bukhari.json holds only the index of the 97 books; the narrations themselves are fetched over the network.

So a hadith a day needs either a connection, or the thirty to be chosen and stored before the month starts — which is the better answer anyway, because the masjid picks them.

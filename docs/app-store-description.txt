The official app of Taiyabah Masjid, Bolton — built for the congregation, and for anyone who needs the masjid's times while they are out.

PRAYER TIMES FROM THE MASJID ITSELF
Beginning and jamaat times come straight from Taiyabah Masjid's own official Salah timetable — the same one printed and put up each year. They are not calculated estimates, so the time on your phone is the time the jamaat actually stands. See today at a glance, or open the full timetable for any date in the year.

NOTIFICATIONS YOU CHOOSE
Be told when jamaat is about to begin, with time to get there. You can also turn on:
• Janazah (funeral prayer) announcements
• Timetable changes, Ramadan and Eid
• Events, bayaans, classes and community gatherings
Each is a separate switch, so you get only what you asked for.

QIBLA
Find the direction of prayer from wherever you are standing.

THE HOLY QUR'AN
The complete mushaf, laid out clearly, with pinch to zoom for closer reading.

LISTEN LIVE
Hear the masjid live when you cannot be there in person.

ZAKAT CALCULATOR
A plain-English guide to who pays zakat and when; the nisab in both gold and silver, worked out from today's prices; and a step-by-step calculator covering cash, gold, silver, business stock and money owed to you.

MADRASAH
The madrasah has taught the community's children since 1967. Admissions, fees, class times, and the full year calendar of closures alongside the Islamic dates.

SERVICES
• Nikah, in keeping with authentic Islamic tradition
• Funeral services, and support for families through every part of an Islamic funeral
• Hall and room hire at the Taiyabah Centre
• Donate securely to the masjid

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
English, Urdu, Arabic and Gujarati, with full right-to-left layout.

Taiyabah Masjid is run by Bolton Central Islamic Society.

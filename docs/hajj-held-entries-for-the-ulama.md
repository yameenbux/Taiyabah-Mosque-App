# The seven entries waiting on the ʿulamā

**For the trustees to take to the ʿulamā. Nothing here is published.**

Seven entries in the Hajj & ʿUmrah section are written but held. They are held
because their `by` field is empty, and an entry with no `by` is invisible: the
importer keeps it out of the app bundle, the website never receives it, and it
cannot open its branch whatever the release switch says. That is working as
intended — this sheet exists to end it, not to work around it.

**Why they were not drafted like the rest.** Everything else in the section was
drafted from a published guide and then read and approved. These seven could
not be, for one of two reasons: either the guide the branch was drafted from
does not address the question at all, or the answer carries consequences heavy
enough that a draft would be a liability rather than a help. They are the
questions a pilgrim asks when something has already gone wrong.

**What is needed from the ʿulamā is the wording itself**, not a yes to a draft.
Where a question below has no proposed text, that is deliberate: proposing an
answer to "what expiation is owed" and asking for a tick invites the tick.

**What is needed from the trustees** is the `by` field — the name of whoever
stands behind the published words. Today every live entry carries
`by: "Taiyabah Masjid"`, because the trustees publish on the charity's
authority.

## 1–3. The three `dam` entries

**Where they appear:** Iḥrām → *If something was broken*
**Branch section id:** `ihram-dam`

This whole section is dark. All three entries are held, so the section does not
render at all — a pilgrim who has broken something in iḥrām currently finds
nothing in the app about it.

### `i-dam-what` — "What a dam is"

> What it is, where it is given, and what the alternatives are for somebody who
> cannot.

Needed: what a dam is, where it must be offered, and what someone does who
cannot afford it or cannot arrange it.

### `i-dam-when` — "Dam, ṣadaqah, or nothing owed"

> The practical table the ʿulamā should supply: the common slips, and which of
> the three each one falls under. This is the entry the whole branch exists for.

Needed: a list of the common slips — perfume, cutting hair or nails, covering
the head, stitched clothing, missing a wājib — and for each, whether it carries
a dam, a ṣadaqah, or nothing. A table is fine; the app renders a list.

**This is the one that matters most.** It is the question people actually ask.

### `i-dam-ask` — "When to ask rather than assume"

> Nobody should work this out from an app while standing in Minā. The masjid's
> number belongs in this entry.

**This entry asserts no ruling at all**, which is why it is the only one with a
proposed text. It says "do not guess, ring us". If the ʿulamā and trustees are
content with it, it can be approved as it stands:

> If you think something may have broken your iḥrām, do not settle it from a
> phone while you are standing there. What is owed depends on what was done,
> when, whether it was deliberate, and whether it was repeated — and those are
> questions for a person, not a list.
>
> Ring the masjid. The number works from Saudi Arabia: **+44 1204 535 997**.
> If it is not urgent, email **info@taiyabahmasjid.com** and it will be put in
> front of the ʿulamā.
>
> Nobody has ever been blamed for asking. Guessing is how a small slip becomes
> an unperformed rite.

*Note for whoever approves it:* the number is printed here in international
form because the reader is abroad. Please confirm that is the right number to
give a pilgrim in Makkah, and whether there is an out-of-hours route.

## 5. `i-intimacy` — "Intimacy"

**Where it appears:** Iḥrām → the restrictions

> The consequences here are graver than a dam and differ by stage. This entry
> must not go live until the ʿulamā have written it in full.

Needed: the ruling at each stage — before the first ṭawāf, between the stages of
Hajj, after the first taḥallul — and what is owed in each case, including where
the Hajj itself is affected.

**This is the single most consequential entry in the section.** The note in the
file asking that it not go live until written in full was put there deliberately
and should be honoured.

## 6. `i-w-hands` — "Hands and feet" (women in iḥrām)

**Where it appears:** Iḥrām → the section for women

> Still open. The restriction on a woman in iḥrām is the face, and the hands and
> feet are not treated the same way — but whether gloves and socks may be worn,
> and whether that changes during ṭawāf, is the ʿulamā's to state. The guide
> this branch was drafted from does not address it.

Needed: whether gloves may be worn, whether socks or covered footwear may be
worn, and whether either answer changes during ṭawāf or saʿī.

This is a known point of difference between the madhhabs, so please state the
position the masjid is giving rather than the range.

## How to put an answer back into the app

For each entry, the developer needs two things:

1. **The text**, in English, as it should appear on screen.
2. **The name for the `by` field** — who stands behind it. Today that is
   `Taiyabah Masjid` on every live entry.

An entry becomes visible on both the app and the website the moment it has
both. No release switch has to be thrown and no separate approval step exists:
`by` *is* the approval.

**Please read the body before naming an approver on it.** The field is easy to
set and the gate's refusal is not a formality — it is the last thing standing
between a note written for a scholar and a pilgrim reading it as a ruling. That
mistake has been made on this file before.

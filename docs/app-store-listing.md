# App Store listing — Taiyabah Masjid

Copy for the "Prepare for Submission" page in App Store Connect. Every claim
below is checked against what the app actually does; App Review rejects
descriptions that promise features the build does not have.

---

## Promotional Text  (max 170 characters)

Prayer times taken straight from the masjid's own timetable, with jamāʿah
notifications, Qibla, the Qurʾān, live audio and a zakat calculator.

---

## Description  (max 4000 characters)

The official app of Taiyabah Masjid, Bolton — built for the congregation, and
for anyone who needs the masjid's times while they are out.

PRAYER TIMES FROM THE MASJID ITSELF
Beginning and jamāʿah times come straight from Taiyabah Masjid's own official
Salah timetable — the same one printed and put up each year. They are not
calculated estimates, so the time on your phone is the time the jamāʿah
actually stands. See today at a glance, or open the full timetable for any
date in the year.

NOTIFICATIONS YOU CHOOSE
Be told when jamāʿah is about to begin, with time to get there. You can also
turn on:
• Janāzah (funeral prayer) announcements
• Timetable changes, Ramadan and Eid
• Events, bayaans, classes and community gatherings
Each is a separate switch, so you get only what you asked for.

QIBLA
Find the direction of prayer from wherever you are standing.

THE HOLY QURʾĀN
The complete muṣḥaf, laid out clearly, with pinch to zoom for closer reading.

LISTEN LIVE
Hear the masjid live when you cannot be there in person.

ZAKAT CALCULATOR
A plain-English guide to who pays zakat and when; the nisab in both gold and
silver, worked out from today's prices; and a step-by-step calculator covering
cash, gold, silver, business stock and money owed to you.

MADRASAH
The madrasah has taught the community's children since 1967. Admissions, fees,
class times, and the full year calendar of closures alongside the Islamic
dates.

SERVICES
• Nikāḥ, in keeping with authentic Islamic tradition
• Funeral services, and support for families through every part of an Islamic
  funeral
• Hall and room hire at the Taiyabah Centre
• Donate securely to the masjid

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
English, Urdu, Arabic and Gujarati, with full right-to-left layout.

Taiyabah Masjid is run by Bolton Central Islamic Society.

## App Review Information

Sign-in required: NO. The madrasah member area says "You will be able to sign
in here", so nothing in the build is behind a login and no demo account is
needed. If that changes before submission, a demo account becomes mandatory.

Notes for the reviewer:

  Taiyabah Masjid is a mosque in Bolton, United Kingdom. This app serves its
  congregation.

  Prayer times are the masjid's own published timetable, not calculated, so
  they are correct for this masjid specifically.

  Donations are voluntary contributions to a place of worship and fund the
  masjid's running costs. They are not payment for any digital content,
  feature or service within the app, and nothing in the app is unlocked by
  donating, so they are handled outside in-app purchase.

  Location is used only to work out the Qibla direction on the device. It is
  not stored or transmitted.

## Screenshots — REQUIRED, and the most likely thing to hold up submission

At least one iPhone 6.9" set is mandatory (1320 x 2868 or 1290 x 2796
portrait). iPad screenshots are required only if the app is offered on iPad.

Good candidates, in order:
  1. Home — today's times and the next jamāʿah
  2. Full timetable
  3. Qibla
  4. The Qurʾān, open on a page
  5. Zakat calculator
  6. Notification choices

Do not show a loading state, an empty screen, or a time that has obviously
passed. Status bar should look clean.

What the app is used for

Counts of requests the masjid's own server already receives — a page of the muṣḥaf, a poster, a gold rate. Nobody is counted. There is no identifier of any kind in these figures: not an address, not a device, not a session. A row is a date, a feature, a platform and a number, and individual requests are never stored. This is what lets the app keep saying there is no tracking of any kind.

Reading the Qur'ān, the du'ā collections, the qibla and the zakat calculator happens on the phone, so most of it is invisible here by design. What shows is what had to come over the wire anyway.

Reports from congregants

Sent from the app's Having trouble screen. Each one carries what that phone could see about itself at the time, so a problem can be read rather than guessed at.

Asks OneSignal’s own servers what they hold for one device — the categories it is signed up to, and whether its subscription is still live. The app shows its subscription id under Alerts → Having trouble?. This is the only view that isn’t the phone’s own copy.

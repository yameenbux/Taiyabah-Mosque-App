Privacy Notice

What the Taiyabah Masjid app collects, why it collects it, who it is shared with, and how long it is kept. Last updated 9 September 2026.

Who we are

Bolton Central Islamic Society, a charity registered in England and Wales, number 1041569, known as Taiyabah Masjid. Our registered address is 31a Draycott Street, Bolton BL1 8HD.

We are the data controller for the information described here. If you have any question about this notice, or want us to delete something we hold about you, ring the office on 01204 535 997 or email info@taiyabahmasjid.com.

The short version

Most of the app asks nothing of you. Prayer times, the Qur’an, the du’ā collections, the qibla compass, the zakat calculator and the mosque information all work without an account and without sending anything about you anywhere.

Three things do involve your information: booking the hall, requesting a nikāḥ date, and turning on notifications. Each is described below.

There is no advertising in the app, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. We do not profile you, we do not sell anything about you, and nothing you do in the app is used to build a picture of you.

What we collect, and why

Booking the hall

When you send a hall booking we receive your first name, surname, address and telephone number, along with the date you want, how many halls, and whether you want the kitchen. We need these to hold the date and for the office to call you to confirm it and arrange access.

Our lawful basis is performance of a contract — you are asking to hire a room, and we cannot arrange that without knowing who you are and how to reach you.

Requesting a nikāḥ date

When you send a nikāḥ request we receive the name, telephone number and email address of the person making the request, whether they are the bride, the groom or a family member, the dates and prayer times that would suit, an estimate of numbers, and anything you choose to write in the notes box. The office rings you to discuss it.

Our lawful basis is consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time by ringing the office, and we will delete the request.

Notifications

If you turn on prayer reminders, your device is registered with OneSignal, our notification provider. They hold an anonymous identifier for your device and the preferences you chose — whether you want jamā’ah reminders, how many minutes before, and whether you want janāzah, announcement and event notices. Your name is not sent, and nothing identifies you personally.

Our lawful basis is consent. Turning notifications off in the app, or in your phone’s settings, ends it.

Payments

Hall deposits, nikāḥ fees and donations are handled by Stripe on their own pages, which open outside the app. Your card details never pass through the app and we never see them. We receive confirmation that a payment was made and the reference it relates to. Stripe is the data controller for the payment itself, under their own privacy policy.

What stays on your phone

These never leave your device and we cannot see them:

  • Your chosen language, text size and notification preferences.
  • Your Qur’ān bookmark, favourites and the page you last read.
  • Downloaded content — language packs and Qur’ān pages — so the app works offline.

Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it removes all of them.

Location

The qibla compass can use your location to point more accurately from exactly where you are standing. It asks first, only when you open the compass and press start, and the position is used on your phone and immediately discarded. It is never stored and never sent to us or to anybody else. If you refuse, the compass still works, pointing from the masjid.

Who else is involved

We use a small number of suppliers to run the app. They act on our instructions and may not use your information for their own purposes.

Supabase
Stores hall bookings and nikāḥ requests. Hosted in the EU.
OneSignal
Delivers notifications. Holds a device identifier and your notification preferences only.
Stripe
Takes payments on its own pages. We never see card details.
Cloudflare
Serves the app and runs the small service that saves your notification preferences.
Google Fonts
Serves the typefaces. Your device requests the font files directly.
YouTube
Plays embedded videos, using the no-cookie player so nothing is set unless you press play.

The zakat calculator fetches gold and silver prices from public price services. Those requests carry nothing about you.

We do not sell or share your information with anyone else, and we will only hand it to a public authority where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Hall bookings
Kept for the booking and for six years afterwards, as charity accounting rules require.
Nikāḥ requests
Deleted twelve months after the request is dealt with, unless a nikāḥ took place, in which case the marriage record is kept as a permanent register.
Notification preferences
Until you turn notifications off or uninstall the app.
Requests that go nowhere
An unpaid hall booking releases its hold after thirty minutes and is deleted.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete it, where we have no continuing reason to keep it;
  • stop using it for a particular purpose;
  • withdraw your consent, where consent is what we relied on.

Ring the office on 01204 535 997 or email info@taiyabahmasjid.com. We will answer within one month, and there is no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would rather you spoke to us first, so we can put it right.

Children

The app is for anyone to use, and nothing in it is aimed at children specifically. The hall booking and nikāḥ forms are for adults, and we do not knowingly collect information from a child through the app. Madrasah admissions are handled separately, on paper and through the office, and are not part of this app.

Changes to this notice

If we change what the app collects we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be announced in the app.

This notice is written in English. Translations of the summary are offered inside the app for convenience; where they differ, the English text is the one that applies.
